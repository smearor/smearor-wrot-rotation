Enum FillStrategy 

Source 
pub enum FillStrategy {
    Fit,
    Cover,
    Clamp,
}
Expand description

How the fill allocation resolves rotation angles where the exact unrotated_bounds solve is infeasible: the bounding-box aspect a rotated rectangle can produce is bounded by tan θ/cot θ, so a viewport aspect outside that range admits no rect whose rotated bounds exactly equal the allocation.

Variants§

§

Fit

Aspect-preserving inscribed fit: the child keeps its natural aspect and shrinks to the largest rect whose rotated bounds fit inside the allocation (unrotated_fit). Letterboxes inside the infeasible band - the original fill fallback behavior.

§

Cover

Aspect-preserving cover: the child keeps its natural aspect and grows to the smallest rect whose rotated bounds contain the allocation - the max-sided counterpart of unrotated_fit. Covers without collapsing, but snaps at the band edge and overhangs the allocation on one axis.

§

Clamp

Aspect-clamped cover: the child aspect follows the exact solve’s aspect clamped to a bounded range around its natural aspect. Covering with the exact aspect reproduces the exact solve wherever it is feasible, so the allocation stays continuous across the whole rotation - the child morphs bar-to-square-to-bar instead of jumping. Always covers the allocation.
